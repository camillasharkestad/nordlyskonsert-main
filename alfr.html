Current focus

ÁLFR — a Northern Lights Concert

ÁLFR is a scenic concert experience where majestic music, spectacular images and the magic of the northern lights meet.

With visuals by Eilif Stene and music by Jørn Lavoll and Marianne Juvik Sæbø, the concert is inspired by Norwegian nature, light and atmosphere.

ÁLFR has been performed at Forum Scene Bergen with the Royal Norwegian Navy Band, pianist and choir, conducted by Bjørn Sagstad, and at KIPO on Askøy. Marianne Juvik Sæbø is the vocalist.

A new ÁLFR concert is now being planned. The date has not been set yet — get in touch if you would like to bring ÁLFR to your venue.

About Marianne

Marianne Juvik Sæbø

Marianne Juvik Sæbø is an experienced and versatile singer based on Askøy, known for her warm voice, musical presence and ability to create moments that feel personal, beautiful and memorable.

With classical training from Bergen and London, she brings both quality and sensitivity to every performance. Her repertoire ranges from hymns, folk music and gospel to classical works, musical theatre and opera.

Marianne can be booked for concerts, church ceremonies, funerals, memorial services and private events. She works closely with each client to choose music that suits the occasion — whether the atmosphere should be solemn, comforting, joyful or grand.

Her current spotlight is ÁLFR: a Nordic concert experience where music, storytelling and visual landscapes meet in an atmospheric universe inspired by nature and the northern lights.
